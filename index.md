# The Odd Archive

**The Odd Archive** is a YouTube channel ([@realoddarchive](https://www.youtube.com/@realoddarchive)) publishing short videos about strange but true moments in history, built from public-domain archives: old newspapers, photographs, engravings and official records. Every video lists its sources.

## Odd Archive Uploader

*Odd Archive Uploader* is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube and schedules their publication time, using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
